**Future Forecast 2025: Which Predictions Actually Came True?**

The survey revealed that many predictions were accurate: a Black U.S. president (Barack Obama, elected 2008), legal and widespread same-sex marriage in the U.S., and the emergence of a deadly new disease (foreshadowing the COVID-19 pandemic) were correctly anticipated. Conversely, predictions that women would become U.S. president by 2025, that most businesses would vanish due to e-commerce, and that humans would routinely live to 100 years old with cures for cancer and AIDS were incorrect. Coates’ technological forecasts were remarkably precise: he accurately predicted ubiquitous internet access, the rise of virtual communities, and AI systems that could mimic or surpass human intelligence. However, Coates was wrong in predicting that genetics would fully decode the human body by 2025, that personalized genetically modified food would be widespread, and that a global middle class, international government bodies, or a single global currency would emerge by 2025—developments that have not occurred and may even be moving in the opposite direction.
