Research Summary
The collected reports, published between September 15 and 16, 2025, detail a series of premature leaks—primarily via an unlisted YouTube video on Meta’s own channel—that preview Meta’s next-generation smart glasses lineup ahead of its Connect event on September 17. Across sources such as El Español, TimesNow, Business Insider, 9to5Mac, and others, the core leak centers on the “Meta Ray-Ban Display,” a second-generation Ray-Ban Meta model featuring a built-in monocular HUD over the right lens capable of turn-by-turn navigation, real-time translation, messaging, and Meta AI voice commands. This unit pairs with a “Hypernova” wristband using surface electromyography (sEMG) to detect hand gestures. Reports note a thicker frame design (weighing about 70 g versus 50 g previously), a roughly $800 price estimate, and a planned mass release in 2026. Additionally, an Oakley Sphaera variant with a camera on the nose bridge was revealed, expanding Meta’s partnership with EssilorLuxottica into sports-oriented AR wearables.
