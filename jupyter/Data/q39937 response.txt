Research Summary
French authorities ordered the immediate closure on September 16, 2025, of a Burger King franchise in Lure, Haute-Saône, after health inspections uncovered multiple cases of food-borne intoxication, pervasive ‘noxious odors’ and serious hygiene breaches—ranging from unclean premises and faulty refrigeration to staff’s inability to ensure food safety. The prefecture’s decision, justified by “faits graves susceptibles de présenter un risque pour la santé des consommateurs,” stipulates that the restaurant remain closed until the Departmental Directorate of Employment, Work, Solidarity and Population Protection (DDETSPP) verifies completion of a comprehensive corrective plan. Burger King France, which operates some 580 outlets nationwide, has launched an internal investigation and pledged full cooperation with state services, including deep-cleaning, equipment upgrades and enhanced staff training.
On the investment side, Bank of New York Mellon (NYSE: BK) has received upbeat analyst attention: Argus named it one of “10 of the Best Stocks to Buy for 2025,” assigning a $105 price target based on 8 % Pershing revenue growth and positive operating leverage, and Morgan Stanley raised its BK target from $95 to $101 with an “overweight” rating, citing potential upside and a consensus “Moderate Buy.” These forecasts reflect expectations of continued earnings strength and healthy deposit and fee trends for the custodian bank.

Intoxications and 'noxious odors': a Burger King closed in Franche-Comté
On September 16, 2025, French state services ordered the closure of a Burger King restaurant in Lure, Haute‑Saône, after several cases of food‑borne intoxication and inspections that revealed serious hygiene violations and 'noxious odors'. Prefect Serge Jacob explained that the measure was justified by 'faits graves, susceptibles de présenter un risque pour la santé des consommateurs' (serious facts that could pose a health risk to consumers). Inspectors found 'absence d'hygiène des locaux, des équipements et des enceintes réfrigérées', 'odeurs nauséabondes dans les locaux de préparation' and an 'incapacité du personnel à assurer la salubrité des denrées alimentaires'. The restaurant, located in a commercial zone on the outskirts of the 8,000‑inhabitant town, will not reopen until the Departmental Directorate of Employment, Work, Solidarity and Population Protection (DDETSPP) confirms that all corrective measures have been implemented. Burger King France, which operates about 580 outlets nationwide, said 'Nous suivons avec la plus grande attention la situation du restaurant de notre franchisé de Lure' and pledged to 'réaffirmer notre volonté de collaborer avec les services de l'État pour résoudre cette situation au plus vite et proposer à nos clients une expérience conforme à nos exigences élevées en termes de qualité et de sécurité'. An internal investigation is underway, and the group has launched a complementary action plan that includes a 'nettoyage complet des cuisines et du restaurant', a 'remise en conformité des installations' and 'un accompagnement renforcé de l'équipe sur place sur les aspects de formation'.

Burger King Closed in Franche-Comté Over Health Concerns

Burger King China to Close Underperforming Locations and Expand in Asia
Burger King China plans to close some underperforming locations and reduce its total number of stores in the country by 2025. To mitigate the impact, the company will open 40-60 new restaurants in strategic locations. Burger King's parent company, RBI Group, has announced plans to close some unprofitable restaurants in the next 12 months, with a threshold of average annual sales below $300,000. However, RBI Group's CEO Josh Kobza emphasized that the company has seen positive initial results since acquiring Burger King China, with local management team's efforts leading to increased sales. Despite the challenges, RBI Group plans to expand Burger King in Asia, with 3,000 new locations planned in the next five years, half of which will be in China. As of 2024, Burger King had 1,474 locations in China.
