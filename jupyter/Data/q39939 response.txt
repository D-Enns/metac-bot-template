The S&P 500 index rose 23.62 points, or 0.04%, to 5,912.17, while the Nasdaq composite index rose 74.93 points, or 0.39%, to 19,175.87. Apple (AAPL) fell 0.2%, while Tesla (TSLA) rose 0.4%. Amazon (AMZN) rose 0.5%. However, Best Buy (BBY) fell 7.3% after lowering its full-year sales forecast. Salesforce (CRM) fell 3.3% after its earnings report. HP (HPQ) fell 8.3% after lowering its full-year profit forecast. The US dollar index fluctuated around 99.95, with a small increase in the early session, but then fell to 99.38, down about 0.49% from the previous trading day. The dollar-yen exchange rate fluctuated between 144 and 146.2, with a high of 146.2 and a low of 144, before falling 0.45% to 114.19. The euro-dollar exchange rate fell to 1.1210, but then rose to 1.1384, before falling to 1.137. The price of gold has been fluctuating between $3260 and $3350, with a recent high of $3345.68. The price of oil has been falling due to concerns about global economic growth, with the price of NYMEX crude oil falling 1.47% to $60.93 per barrel, and the price of Brent crude oil falling 1.49% to $63.36 per barrel.
