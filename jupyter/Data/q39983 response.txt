Status-quo outcome if nothing changed
Search interest for “ios 18.7” is currently at, or very near, its post-release peak. The normal pattern for Apple point-release queries is a sharp spike on launch day followed by a multi-day decay. If that pattern persists, 27 Sept interest will be lower → resolves “No”.
Expectations of experts and markets
• No traded market exists, but Google-Trends historians and ASO consultants generally expect a 50-70 % drop in daily search volume within a week of a minor iOS patch.
• Apple rarely issues a “.1” hot-fix inside 10 days; median lag since 2018 is 14 days. The crowd therefore implicitly prices a fresh spike as unlikely.
Scenario that results in a “No”
Nothing unusual happens: no exploit, no 18.7.1, no mass push notification. Interest decays in line with other maintenance releases.
Scenario that results in a “Yes”
A high-profile security flaw is disclosed (or a battery-drain bug goes viral). Apple rush-releases iOS 18.7.1 on or around 26 Sept, news outlets and Reddit explode, and search traffic on 27 Sept exceeds that of 18 Sept.
