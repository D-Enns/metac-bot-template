Pentair (PNR) Receives Downgrade from TD Cowen Post Q2 Report |
Pentair (PNR) has been downgraded from Buy to Hold by TD Cowen, with a revised price target of $110 from the previous $115. This decision is based on the lack of expected growth in short-term estimates without a market recovery. Analysts suggest maintaining a neutral position on PNR shares due to the stock's relative valuation approaching historic highs. Despite this, Pentair delivered a record quarter across all four metrics: sales, adjusted operating income, return on sales, and adjusted EPS. The company generated record free cash flow and repurchased $75 million of shares in Q2. Pentair increased its full-year 2025 guidance, expecting sales growth of approximately 1% to 2% and adjusted EPS to be approximately $4.75 to $4.85, up 11% at the midpoint versus full-year 2024. According to GuruFocus estimates, the estimated GF Value for Pentair PLC (PNR, Financial) in one year is $69.63, suggesting a downside of 33.6% from the current price of $104.87.
