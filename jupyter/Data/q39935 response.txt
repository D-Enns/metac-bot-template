Multiple recent polls show Zohran Mamdani as a clear front-runner in the November 4, 2025 New York City mayoral election. A Quinnipiac University survey (4–8 September, n=967, ±3.9%) gives him 45% support—22 points ahead of Andrew Cuomo at 23%—and a 45% positive vs. 36% negative favorability. A New York Times/Siena College poll (Sept. 2–6, ±3.6%) finds Mamdani at 46% in a four-candidate field (vs. Cuomo 24%, Curtis Sliwa 15%, Eric Adams 9%) and leading 48% to 44% in a head-to-head matchup with Cuomo. Similar margins appear in Fox News–reported and Guardian Angels-co-founder Curtis Sliwa question scenarios. These data are bolstered by Governor Kathy Hochul’s endorsement on September 15, 2025—citing shared views on affordability, policing and opposition to Trump—at a time when Mamdani leads other major candidates with roughly 43–45% support.
Mamdani secured the Democratic nomination on June 24 with 56% of ranked-choice votes, overcoming former Gov. Andrew Cuomo by 12 points. He has attracted endorsements from progressive figures including Bernie Sanders and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez. Fact-checking by PesaCheck confirms that claims of his already having been elected are false, noting that the general election is still pending. Incumbent Mayor Eric Adams, Cuomo (as an independent), and Republican Curtis Sliwa remain in the race, while some observers—including former President Trump—have suggested the contest could tighten if any rivals withdraw.
