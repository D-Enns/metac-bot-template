He argues that the West is experiencing a new form of 'Scavenger' that is united in its hatred of Western civilization. Shapiro emphasizes that this is not just anti-Semitism, but a broader hatred of the West that requires a united response. He urges readers to 'be a civilization of Lions' and to teach their children to fight against the 'Scavengers'.
