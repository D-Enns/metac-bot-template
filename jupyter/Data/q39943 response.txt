Fundery to Turn Profitable in FY 2026 with Strong Core Business, Plans 2‑Share Split Effective Oct 1

The company projects a 21.5 % revenue rise to ¥2,999 million for FY 2026 (ending March 2026) and a turnaround to an operating profit of ¥86 million from a ¥1,330 million loss the previous year.  Earnings forecasts for the full year remain unchanged, with a 10 % revenue increase to ¥2,167 million for MFD, a 154 % rise to ¥3,980 million for CID, and a 33 % rise to ¥5,200 million for marketing, while operating losses for CID shrink to ¥285 million.  The company attributes the upside to price revisions (e.g., a 11.3 % average increase across all categories in July 2025), accelerated retail sales, and expanded distribution networks, including 3,000 new retail outlets planned for Q2 2025.  Fundery also announced a 2‑share split effective 1 October 2025 and a “Fan Shareholder 20,000” initiative to boost liquidity and shareholder engagement.  The stock has steadily lifted its lower price bound; the closing price on 12 September 2025 was ¥475, with a projected PER of ~112× and a market cap of ~¥3.1 billion.
