Research Summary
Apple began rolling out iOS 26 as a free update on September 15–16, 2025, for all iPhones from the 11 series onward (including iPhone SE 2020/2022) and iPadOS 26 on compatible iPads. Across more than a dozen news outlets, the update is consistently described as introducing a “Liquid Glass” translucent interface, 3-D spatial lock-screen options, and streamlined navigation in core apps (Camera, Safari, Photos). Apple Intelligence features—live translation in Messages, FaceTime and Phone; Genmoji; Visual Intelligence; Workout Buddy; Hold Assist and voicemail triage—are flagged as major enhancements, though AI-dependent functions require A17-class chips (iPhone 15 Pro and later). The release also unified Apple’s OS numbering (iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26, watchOS 26, tvOS 26, visionOS 26) and added new apps (Apple Games) and privacy tools (call screening, sender filters, visited-places in Maps).
Device compatibility and update procedures are uniformly detailed: iOS 26 supports iPhone 11 and newer (dropping XR/XS/XS Max), with unsupported models receiving incremental iOS 18 updates; installation via Settings > General > Software Update; Wi-Fi and ≥50 % battery recommended; and backups advised. Reports note potential launch-day download delays due to demand but emphasize the factual, non-opinionated nature of coverage.

Apple Releases New iOS 26 for iPhones; What Changes in the System and Which Versions Will Be Updated
Apple began rolling out the free iOS 26 update on Monday, September 15, 2025, to all iPhones from the 11 series onward. The most noticeable change is the new ‘Liquid Glass’ visual style, described by Apple as a translucent, glass‑like interface that adapts to surrounding content and lighting conditions; the company said, 'O design é translúcido e se comporta como vidro no mundo real. Sua cor é influenciada pelo conteúdo ao redor e se adapta de forma inteligente a ambientes claros e escuros'.
Other new features include a 3‑D lock‑screen option that gives depth to background images, enhanced messaging with background customization, polls, sender filters and typing indicators, a dedicated Apple Games app that showcases new releases and friend challenges, and a streamlined camera app with only two main buttons—Photo and Video.
Apple also announced a shift in its operating‑system numbering: iOS 26 will replace iOS 19, and the same version number will apply across all Apple platforms (iPadOS 26, macOS 26, watchOS 26, visionOS 26, tvOS 26).
The update brings several Apple Intelligence AI features, such as live translation in Messages, FaceTime and Phone, Hold Assist for call waiting, voicemail‑based call triage, Workout Buddy that offers motivational tips, emoji creation via Genmoji, and screen‑visualization that can add calendar events from screenshots.
Supported devices are all iPhones from the 11 series to the latest 16 models, including the iPhone SE (3rd generation). AI‑dependent features are limited to the iPhone 16 line and the iPhone 15 Pro/15 Pro Max.
To install, users go to Settings > General > Software Update; automatic updates will download over Wi‑Fi.

Apple Launches iOS 26 with Liquid Glass Interface and New Features
Apple officially launched iOS 26 for iPhone users on 15 September 2025, following a beta release in the summer. The update arrives alongside a bundle of new operating systems for iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro, marking the largest update package in Apple’s history. According to the site 'Thomas Jaid', iOS 26 can only be installed on iPhone 11 or newer models, plus the iPhone SE 2020 and 2022. The update removes software support for the iPhone XR, XS, and XS Max, encouraging users of those devices to upgrade.
The headline feature is the 'Liquid Glass' interface, which gives the system a more transparent and fluid look while keeping core menus in their usual places. Some reports note that the translucent elements may appear inconsistent, suggesting users adjust transparency settings.
Key app redesigns include the Camera, Safari, and Messages. The Camera now has a simplified layout with clear buttons for basic shooting modes and side‑scrollable advanced modes such as Portrait and Video. Safari receives a tighter tab layout with an option to revert to the classic view via settings. Messages gains a filter for unknown senders, a poll feature in group chats, and new background customization options.
Other new features are:
'Screen Incoming Calls', which forces unknown callers to identify themselves before the call is forwarded, helping block spam.
'Visited Places' in Maps, initially available in the U.S., U.K., Canada, Australia, Malaysia, and Switzerland.
Apple Intelligence translation, available only on iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
Spatial Scenes on the lock screen, requiring at least an iPhone 12.
Apple also announced the iPhone 17 series—four variants (iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max)—at its annual fall event, with worldwide sales beginning 19 September 2025. The company advises users to back up data before updating and to review supported devices and regional features.
Overall, iOS 26 introduces a major visual overhaul with 'Liquid Glass', revamped core apps, and several new privacy‑focused utilities, positioning it as one of Apple’s most talked‑about updates in recent years.

The Games app consolidates downloads, progress and purchases, reinforcing Apple’s mobile gaming presence.
Performance gains are evident on recent iPhones, with lower resource consumption and faster routine tasks. Compatibility is limited to devices with an A13 Bionic chip or newer; iPhones released from 2019 onward (iPhone 11, SE 2/3, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17 families) can install iOS 26. Support for older models ends with the A12 Bionic and earlier chips, including iPhone XR, XS, XS Max, X, 8/8 Plus, 7/7 Plus, the first‑generation SE and the entire iPhone 6s/6/6 Plus line. These devices will not receive new features or future security patches.
