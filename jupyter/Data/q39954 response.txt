U21s report: Arsenal 1-0 Brighton & Hove Albion
Arsenal Under‑21s secured their first three points of the Premier League 2 season with a 1‑0 win over Brighton & Hove Albion at the Mangata Developments Stadium on 29 August 2025. The match was dominated by Arsenal’s possession, with Ife Abraham described as 'crucial in conducting the play' and contributing to several key moments. A late goal from Charles Sagnoe Jnr, who crossed from the left wing, finished the game after a series of attempts from both sides, including a header by Nti that was denied by the backline. Arsenal’s defense remained solid, preventing any goals from Brighton. The victory also gave Arsenal their first clean sheet of the campaign. The next fixture is a Premier League 2 match against Wolverhampton Wanderers on 20 September 2025 at the same venue, with kickoff at 1 pm UK time.
