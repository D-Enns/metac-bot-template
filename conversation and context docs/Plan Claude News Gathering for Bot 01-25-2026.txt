# News Gathering and Summary for Forecasting Specific Questions

## Purpose
- To gather and process news relevant to questions for **probablistic forecasting*
- The questions are of the type typically forecast by **superforecasters**
- Convert it to a format for bot forecasting useful
- Integrate the news into the forecast bot question forecast

## Gathering News Workflow

### Read the question and background from a forecasting website such as Metaculus.com
- Consider all information and links on the question website relevant information

### Produce one or more prompts to gather potentially important news
- Prioritize sources that provide **quantitative information** on outcomes
- Also useful are news sources that suggest **likelihood** of outcomes 
- Sources indicating the **potential** outcomes
- Sources that show the **current status** of the forecast target outcome
- Sources that indicate the question has already resolved (and how)

### Select the top 5 to 10 news sources
- Based on quality, reliability of source, and uniqueness

## Preparing News for Forecast Bot Use

### Summarize each selected news source into 1 to 2 
- To the extent included in the news source:
	- Include quantitative information useful to forecasting the question outcome
	- Potential outcomes
	- Likely and unlikely outcomes
	- Current status
	- Flag if the **question has already resolved**.
	
## Place the processed news in the template bot and forecasting tools system
- Make the processed news ready for use by the forecasting LLM
