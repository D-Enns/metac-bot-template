# Alternate Method for Aggregating Numeric Forecasts

# Problems

## Problem 1: GPR method doesn't work well for the high and low ranges of the distribution (CDF)
- The CDF needs to be defined from percentile 1 to percentile 99
- the GPR method seems to introduce artifacts at the high and low percentiles (0% - 20%) and (80% - 100%)

## Problem 2: the distribution is lumpy
- Probably due to randomness in individual forecasts

# Tentative Solution

## 1: Aggregate by fitting normal distribution to forecasts
- Linear regression of percentile to cumulative value 
- Cumulative value is linear against percentile (in probit or z scale)

## 2: Alternate: Segmented fit
- Same linear strategy, but fit percentiles 0-50 with one curve and 50-100 with another.

## Why
- By assuming the distribution type, we smooth the curve and project to arbitrarily small or large percentiles.

## Issues
- Normal distribution may be a bad assumption
- Median may not be well picked
- Revisit Binary?
