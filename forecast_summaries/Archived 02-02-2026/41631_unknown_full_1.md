To resolve delays, taxpayers should check the refund or demand status on the income tax e-filing portal, revalidate bank details (including IFSC code), and submit a refund reissue request if needed. Nudge alerts via email or SMS can help identify mismatches and allow voluntary revised returns. The Centralised Processing Centre has until December 31, 2026, to process returns; if delays are not taxpayer-caused, the department must pay interest at 0.5% per month (6% annually). To prevent issues, taxpayers should ensure their PAN is linked with Aadhaar and their bank account is pre-validated on the portal.
