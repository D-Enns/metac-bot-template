**Senate Reaffirms Back Pay For 41 Days Of Missed Wages As Shutdown Nears End**
As the U.S. government shutdown entered its 41st day on November 10, 2025, the Senate passed a stopgap funding bill to reopen federal agencies through January 2026 and reaffirmed retroactive pay for furloughed and essential federal workers under the Government Employee Fair Treatment Act of 2019 (GEFTA). GEFTA, enacted as Public Law 116-1 and signed by President Donald Trump, mandates that all federal employees—both furloughed and essential workers—receive back pay for the period of the funding lapse, starting after December 22, 2018.
