The Centralised Processing Centre has until December 31, 2026, to process returns; if delays are not taxpayer-caused, the department must pay interest at 0.5% per month (6% annually). To prevent issues, taxpayers should ensure their PAN is linked with Aadhaar and their bank account is pre-validated on the portal.
