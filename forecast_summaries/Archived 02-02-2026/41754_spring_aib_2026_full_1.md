Russian President Vladimir Putin announced in February 2023 that Russia would suspend participation but not withdraw from the treaty, stating that Moscow would only resume talks if the agreement accounted for nuclear arsenals of other NATO nuclear powers—Britain and France. In September 2025, Putin said Russia would remain committed to the treaty’s quantitative limits for one additional year, provided the U.S. acted similarly. On October 5, 2025, U.S. President Donald Trump responded to a TASS inquiry by calling Putin’s proposal a 'good idea.'
