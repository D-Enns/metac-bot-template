- Analyses emphasizing “gray-zone pressure,” cyber/disinfo, and ongoing tension without asserting imminent kinetic action.

#### Bucket 3 — points toward a **higher** CP
- Claims (even if opinionated) that China may “test a partial blockade in 2026” ahead of 2027 milestones.

- General “organized chaos” framing suggesting multiple flashpoints and elevated risk appetite/miscalculation.
