**Mint Explainer: How Sebi is planning to change closing price discovery | Stock Market News**
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) has introduced a Closing Auction Session (CAS) framework to replace the current volume-weighted average price (VWAP) method for determining closing prices in the equity cash market. The CAS, set to launch on 3 August 2026, will run for 20 minutes at the end of each trading day, during which all eligible limit and market orders are pooled to determine a single equilibrium price based on the balance of total demand and supply. This change aims to enhance price integrity, reduce manipulation (such as 'marking the close'), and align India’s market structure with global standards used by major exchanges like the New York Stock Exchange. The system includes a random closing time to prevent last-minute order manipulation, a 3% price band to limit sharp price movements, and a fallback mechanism: if no equilibrium price is found, the system defaults to a shorter VWAP, then the last traded price, and finally the previous day’s closing price. Institutional investors, including mutual funds, ETFs, and index funds, will benefit from more accurate closing prices for portfolio valuation and index weighting. Retail investors will also gain from a closing price that better reflects overall market sentiment. Sebi has mandated stock exchanges and clearing corporations to upgrade technology, implement system-driven random closure, enforce surveillance, and issue detailed guidelines. The pre-open session will also be revised and implemented from 7 September 2026. According to S. Ravi, former BSE chairman, CAS is a landmark step toward globally recognized best practices in price discovery and liquidity centralization.
