**Income Tax Refund Stuck? Types of Notices Issued by the Department and How to Reply | LatestLY**
Income tax refunds for the 2024-25 financial year (Assessment Year 2025-26) are experiencing delays, particularly for refunds exceeding INR 30,000, due to stricter verification, data mismatches, and enhanced risk-based scrutiny by the Income Tax Department. The department is using automated data analytics to cross-check income declared in returns with Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement (AIS), flagging even minor discrepancies in bank interest, dividend income, or capital gains. Returns claiming higher deductions not previously disclosed via Form 12BB are also subject to manual review to prevent false claims. Taxpayers may receive one of four notices: a tax demand notice (requiring payment or rectification), a defective return notice (with 15 days to correct errors), a refund adjustment notice (adjusting the refund against a prior-year tax demand), or a scrutiny notice (requiring submission of supporting documents).
