**Resolution of January 15, 2026, by the Treasury and Financial Policy Directorate General: Call for Treasury Liquidity Auction with Daily Maturity**
On January 15, 2026, the Dirección General del Tesoro y Política Financiera issued a resolution calling for a liquidity auction to conduct double purchase-sale operations with daily maturity, valid for 42 days. These operations are authorized under Orden ECE/417/2019, which permits active treasury management operations to temporarily generate returns on state treasury resources. The auction and resulting operations are governed by the Resolution of December 15, 2023, which establishes general conditions for temporary asset acquisition and time deposit operations. The interest rate to be paid is variable, calculated as the sum of the differential offered by the winning entity—either positive or negative—and the euro short-term reference rate (€STR). Each participating entity must submit a bid with a rate at least 2% of the total auctioned amount. In the event of a second round, only entities that participated in and were awarded in the first round may participate, and the 2% minimum bid requirement from the first round does not apply. The resolution was signed by the Director General of Treasury and Financial Policy, with the signature delegated by Resolution of February 29, 2024, and countersigned by the Subdirector General of State Treasury Management, Manuel Varela Bellido.
